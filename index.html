About NovaMind AI

NovaMind AI is a next-generation artificial intelligence assistant built by CreativeStack to empower students, developers, researchers, and business professionals with instant, expert-level intelligence.

Our Mission

Powerful AI should be accessible to everyone. NovaMind AI delivers expert-level answers across education, software engineering, content creation, and strategic business thinking — with zero friction, zero complexity, and zero compromise on quality.

Powered by Frontier Technology

NovaMind AI runs on Groq's revolutionary Language Processing Unit (LPU) infrastructure paired with Meta's Llama 3.3 70B model — delivering response speeds dramatically faster than traditional GPU-based AI, while maintaining the deepest contextual intelligence available today.

What NovaMind AI Can Do

  • Students (K-12 & University): Instant tutoring, essay feedback, concept explanations, and exam preparation across every subject.
  • Developers & Engineers: Generate production-ready code, debug errors, design system architecture, and review code across any language.
  • Business Professionals: Draft strategies, create market analyses, write investor memos, and develop complete pitch decks.
  • Content & Social Media Creators: SEO-optimized articles, viral social content, scripts, brand voice, and growth frameworks.
